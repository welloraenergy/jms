01 · Overview

See the work in progress

Use JMS to keep track of job records, their customers and their current progress.

Job records

Each job brings related quotation, purchase order, delivery, return and invoice details together.

Find and review

Use the available job list and filters to locate work that needs attention or follow-up.

02 · Job Entry

Create a job

Start with the main quotation details and the client.

Choose New Job, enter the quotation reference and date, select the client and fill in the relevant job description or details. Save the job to create its record.

Use the same job when adding later stages, rather than creating a duplicate.

03 · Job Stages

Record progress by stage

Complete each stage when information becomes available. Not every stage needs to be completed at once.

Quotation & Client PO

Record the quotation and the client's purchase order details, including relevant dates, references and terms.

Delivery & Return

Track delivery and return tickets, partial or full movements, and related status information.

Invoice

Record invoice reference, submission dates, amounts and status when billing progresses.

Remarks

Use stage remarks for useful follow-up notes or outstanding matters.
